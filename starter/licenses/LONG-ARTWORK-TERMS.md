## Long artwork terms

Long, the halberd-bearing warlord mecha in `assets/long/`, is drawn from an original design by WindyWin
(the crest of gold horns, the closed mask, the violet crystal blades and red segmented plumes carried on a
backpack frame, the violet chest with its small red core and gold spearhead, the short uneven armoured skirt,
the clawed feet and the long halberd with a detachable red head). It is not based on any existing model kit or
franchise. Every frame is drawn from vector shapes by [`scripts/draw_long.py`](../scripts/draw_long.py), with
the shared kit in [`scripts/pet_art.py`](../scripts/pet_art.py); together they are the art's source.

The character design remains WindyWin's. The frames, the preview and the drawing script's artwork may be
copied and distributed with Agent Pet, changed or not, as part of the app or of a pet pack for it. For any
other use of the design, ask its author. Agent Pet credits WindyWin in About.

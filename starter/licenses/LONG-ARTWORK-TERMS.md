## Long artwork terms

Long, the chibi warlord mecha in `assets/long/`, is WindyWin's original character design and artwork.

**The design.** A gold crown with a cyan gem, a black helm with glowing cyan eyes, layered gold-rimmed pauldrons
with cyan gems, violet crystals behind the shoulders, red segmented tendrils, violet thigh plates, red shin fins
and a long spear with gold fittings. It is not based on any existing model kit or franchise.

**Where the frames come from.** They are made from the designer's painted sheet,
[`scripts/long_art/design-sheet.png`](../scripts/long_art/design-sheet.png):
1. The sheet's four poses are cut out of it (`scripts/long_art/pose1.png` to `pose4.png`).
2. The standing pose is split into the parts of a cutout rig (`scripts/long_art/rig/`).
3. [`scripts/draw_long.py`](../scripts/draw_long.py) poses, relights and adds effects to them.

The effects are drawn by the script; the character in every frame is the designer's own painting.

**What you may do.** The frames, the preview, the sheet, the cut poses and rig parts may be copied and
distributed with Agent Pet, changed or not, as part of the app or of a pet pack for it. For any other use of
the design or the artwork, ask its author. Agent Pet credits WindyWin in About.

## Kuro artwork terms

Kuro, the little ninja pet in `assets/kuro/`, is original artwork made for Agent Pet. Every frame is drawn
from vector shapes by [`scripts/draw_kuro.py`](../scripts/draw_kuro.py), which is the art's source. The
character, its crescent-moon emblem and its props are Agent Pet's own designs and are not taken from any
other work.

The frames, the preview and the drawing script's artwork are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may copy, change and
distribute them for any purpose without asking and without giving credit. Agent Pet credits the
Agent Pet contributors in About.

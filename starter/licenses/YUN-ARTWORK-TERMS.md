## Yun artwork terms

Yun, the little cultivator pet in `assets/yun/`, is original artwork made for Agent Pet in the spirit of
xianxia (cultivation) stories. Every frame is drawn from vector shapes by
[`scripts/draw_yun.py`](../scripts/draw_yun.py), with the shared kit in
[`scripts/pet_art.py`](../scripts/pet_art.py); together they are the art's source. The character and its
props (the flying sword, talismans, cauldron, jade slip and the rest) are Agent Pet's own designs and are not
taken from any other work.

The frames, the preview and the drawing scripts' artwork are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may copy, change and
distribute them for any purpose without asking and without giving credit. Agent Pet credits the
Agent Pet contributors in About.

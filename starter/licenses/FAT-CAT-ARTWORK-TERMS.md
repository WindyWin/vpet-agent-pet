## Orange Fat Cat artwork terms

The Orange Fat Cat artwork is by **shuaa** of [megupets](https://www.megupets.com), published on
OpenGameArt at <https://opengameart.org/content/orange-fat-cat> and dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The pack's readme reads:

> Sprites by shuaa
> www.megupets.com
> CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)
> Public Domain Dedication

CC0 requires no attribution. Agent Pet credits the author anyway, in About and in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Agent Pet's frames are the pack's PNG frames, placed in the idle pose's position on a shared 1000 × 1000
canvas and scaled by one factor so the cat keeps its size between animations. The drawings themselves are
unchanged.
